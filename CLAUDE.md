# AI Engineering Reading List

Curated reading list for engineers integrating AI into production systems. Single-file repo: just `README.md`.

## Thesis

"The bottleneck isn't capability - it's trust." Every entry connects to this. The framework is Observable, Reversible, Enforceable (ORE).

## Structure

The README contains:
- Three reading trails (Bainbridge, Trust, People) that thread through the material
- Sections grouped by theme, not chronology
- Each entry: author, linked title, year, 2-4 sentences on relevance

## Conventions

- British English throughout.
- No em-dashes. Use commas, semicolons, or separate sentences.
- No promotional language. No jargon. Plain, direct descriptions.
- No emojis. No exclamation marks.
- Tone: quiet authority. Writing for busy, sceptical senior engineers.
- Entry format: `**Author - [Title](URL)** (Year)` followed by explanation.
- Cross-reference between entries and to my essays on cronyn.co.uk where relevant.

## What not to do

- Do not add entries without explaining why they matter for production AI trust.
- Do not reorganise sections without preserving the three reading trails.
- Do not duplicate content from my essays; link to them instead.
- Do not add a build step. This is a single markdown file.
